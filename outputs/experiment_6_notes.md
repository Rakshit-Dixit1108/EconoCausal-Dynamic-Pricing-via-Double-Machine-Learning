

\# Experiment 6 – Hyperparameter Tuning



\## Objective

To compare different Random Forest configurations.



\## Configurations Tested

\- 100 trees, min\_samples\_leaf=5

\- 200 trees, min\_samples\_leaf=10

\- 300 trees, min\_samples\_leaf=20



\## Results

The 300-tree configuration achieved the best ROC-AUC,

F1-score, Brier score and cross-validation ROC-AUC mean.



\## Conclusion

The 300-tree configuration performed best among the

three tested configurations. Further validation is required.
